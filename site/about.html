About KaamWithAI

AI for practical operators. We publish practical workflows, tools and real-world stories for people running businesses. Less hype. More useful work.

Who this is for

KaamWithAI is for small-business owners, independent professionals, consultants and small teams who want to make everyday work better without becoming full-time AI researchers. We focus on the jobs that keep a business moving: enquiries, follow-up, quoting, scheduling, finance admin, reporting, customer communication and the handoffs between them.

What we cover

We look at where AI genuinely helps, where ordinary automation is more reliable, and where a person should stay in control. That means comparing AI with spreadsheets, rules, OCR, integrations and existing software—not assuming every problem needs an agent or another subscription.

How we report

We start with a specific business problem, then look for evidence that helps an operator make a decision. We prioritise official product documentation, primary sources, original research, direct practitioner accounts and reproducible tests. We link to important sources, date product and availability claims, and distinguish what a source said from what we infer.

  1. Start with the work. Explain the actual task, the people involved and what a successful outcome looks like.
  2. Check the evidence. Prefer primary documentation and direct sources. Recheck changing product capabilities before publication.
  3. Show the limits. Include failure modes, permissions, review steps and the work that remains human.
  4. Make the takeaway usable. Give readers a decision rule, practical next step or workflow they can adapt—not just a list of tools.

How to read our evidence

  • Hands-on test: We used the product or workflow ourselves and describe the setup, date and limits of the test.
  • Desk research: We reviewed documentation and other sources but did not independently operate the workflow.
  • Self-reported example: A person described their own experience. We attribute the claim and do not present it as independently verified.
  • Illustrative workflow: A worked example designed to explain a process. It is not evidence that a named business uses it or achieved a particular result.
  • Independently verified: We checked a material claim against evidence beyond the original account. We explain what was checked; this label does not imply that every detail of a story was audited.

What we won't claim

  • We do not invent customers, interviews, quotes, author credentials, product tests or savings figures.
  • We do not turn a Reddit post or other personal account into a verified case study.
  • We do not imply that an AI action happened unless there is evidence it completed.
  • We do not recommend giving an AI system more access or authority than the task requires.
  • We do not pretend AI is the answer when a spreadsheet, rule or clearer human process is better.

AI assistance and editorial responsibility

AI tools may assist with research organisation, outlining and editing. Their output is not treated as source evidence. A human editor remains responsible for checking material claims, context and links, and for the final published work. We correct material errors when identified.
