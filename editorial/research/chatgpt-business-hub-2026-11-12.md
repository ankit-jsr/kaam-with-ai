# Editorial research brief: ChatGPT Business Hub and Business Agents

**Status:** Research brief only — not approved for publication  
**Researched:** 12 November 2026  
**Audience:** International English-speaking small-business owners; use the US as the default market, but state availability limits clearly.

## Working headline

**ChatGPT Has a New Business Hub. What Small Business Owners Can Actually Do With It.**

Alternative: **OpenAI Wants Your Business on ChatGPT. Here's What That Actually Changes.**

## Reader question

If customers are beginning to use ChatGPT to discover and compare businesses, what can an owner actually control today — and what is still outside their control?

## Editorial thesis

OpenAI is adding an owner-managed business layer inside ChatGPT: eligible businesses can claim a profile, update information, share product data and see limited aggregate discovery signals. A narrower group of US ecommerce and online-service businesses can also publish a business agent.

This is useful infrastructure for keeping business facts current and understanding some ChatGPT-driven discovery. It is **not** a guaranteed ranking boost, a complete search-analytics product, or an autonomous checkout and booking system. The distinction between a claimed profile, an agent, and ChatGPT's independent recommendations is the article's central point.

## What launched

OpenAI announced Businesses in ChatGPT in beta on **10 November 2026**. Business Hub lets eligible owners or authorized representatives claim and manage a business profile. After verification, they can update business details, connect product feeds and access insights. Business Agents are a separate optional feature for eligible ecommerce and online-service businesses in the US.

## Verified facts to use

### 1. Claiming a profile and publishing an agent are separate

- A business can be claimed by its owner or an authorized representative, subject to eligibility and approval.
- Available verification methods may include business phone, business email, website-domain DNS verification or Shopify.
- After the claim is approved, Persona identity verification is required before the owner can publish profile changes, connect a product feed, view insights or create an eligible agent.
- Claiming a profile does not automatically publish a Business Agent. Publishing the agent is a separate, optional step.
- OpenAI reviews and publishes business-information changes; updates may take time to appear.

### 2. Availability is not universal

- Profile-claim availability depends on business location, business type and supported verification.
- OpenAI's current Help Center says local stores and restaurants can claim only an existing US listing.
- Business Agents are currently limited to eligible ecommerce and online-service businesses in the US with a website and an eligible industry.
- Do not imply that an Indian, UK or other non-US business can currently publish a Business Agent. Do not imply that every business can claim a profile.

### 3. What the profile can contain

- Website, business description, product or service category, logo, delivery policy and return policy.
- Retailers can connect a product feed with prices and availability. Shopify is supported, but Shopify is not mandatory; OpenAI also documents a CSV feed route.
- Connecting a feed does not publish an agent and does not guarantee that ChatGPT recommends the products.

### 4. What the insights actually measure

- Business Hub Insights show aggregate activity over the past 28 days, including mentions, website clicks and discovery topics.
- For an online business, a mention is counted when ChatGPT references the business's website; the brand name alone does not count.
- Discovery topics are grouped themes, not individual customer prompts or a direct measure of purchase intent.
- Businesses do not receive individual prompts or consumer conversations, including Business Agent chats.
- A blank metric may mean activity has not reached a reporting threshold, data is still being collected, or reporting is unavailable. It does not necessarily mean zero activity.

### 5. What a Business Agent can and cannot do today

- It can answer questions about the business's products and policies using information the business supplies, under instructions the business controls.
- The experience is clearly labelled and separate from the user's main ChatGPT conversation.
- It can link customers to the business website.
- It cannot currently complete purchases, make bookings or check an individual customer's order. Those actions happen on the business website or through a separate supported product.
- It must not pretend to be a human or independent adviser, invent products/prices/availability/results, pressure users, collect unnecessary high-risk personal data, provide licensed professional advice or make high-stakes decisions without human review.

### 6. Claiming does not buy ranking

OpenAI explicitly says claiming a business does not guarantee increased visibility or give the business direct control over ChatGPT's independent recommendations. OpenAI also says advertising does not influence independent answers. Keep this distinction prominent; do not call Business Hub a ranking tool or promise an SEO uplift.

## Practical guidance for a small-business owner

1. **Check eligibility before spending time on setup.** Profile claims and Business Agents have different availability rules.
2. **Make the facts accurate first.** Check the business name, website, category, description, logo, service area, delivery and return policies.
3. **Keep product data current.** If selling products, ensure prices and availability in the feed match the storefront.
4. **Treat the agent as a product-and-policy explainer.** Do not expect it to handle checkout, bookings, refunds or order status today.
5. **Test real customer questions before publishing.** Include stock, price, delivery, returns, warranty and edge cases. Fix source information when the answer is wrong.
6. **Measure the right things.** Watch mentions, website clicks and discovery topics as directional signals, not a complete record of customer prompts or all AI-driven discovery.
7. **Keep a human escalation route.** Complex complaints, exceptions, safety issues and decisions with financial or legal consequences should reach a person.

## Suggested story structure

1. **Opening:** A customer asks ChatGPT which business to choose. The owner can now claim a profile and, for some businesses, offer a separate chat with an agent. Establish the new control surface without promising that it changes rankings.
2. **What OpenAI actually launched:** Explain Business Hub, product feeds, insights and Business Agents as distinct pieces.
3. **Who can use it:** Make the US limitation and location/business-type differences clear early.
4. **What a business owner can control:** Accurate profile facts, policies, products and agent instructions.
5. **What they cannot control:** Independent recommendations, ranking, individual prompt data, and completed transactions.
6. **A practical setup checklist:** What to prepare and test before publishing.
7. **Close:** Treat it as a new customer-information channel worth preparing for, not a magic visibility switch.

## Claims to avoid

- “Claiming your business makes ChatGPT recommend you more.”
- “Every business can create a Business Agent.”
- “Business Agents are available worldwide.”
- “The agent can take orders, bookings or payments.”
- “Business owners can see what individual customers asked ChatGPT.”
- “A blank Insights panel means nobody is discovering the business.”
- “This is the new Google Business Profile.” It is a different product with different availability and capabilities.

## Open questions before drafting

- Confirm the current eligible industry list from the Business Agents Policy and whether OpenAI changes it during the rollout.
- Confirm exact profile-claim availability for non-US businesses at the time of publication; do not infer it from the agent rollout.
- If using screenshots, capture the live Business Hub UI only after an eligible account is available; do not fabricate a walkthrough.
- Recheck the Help Center immediately before publication because the rollout is changing quickly.

## Editorial recommendation

**Proceed to a reported practical explainer, not a breaking-news rewrite.** The strongest value is helping an owner understand what they can update, what the metrics mean, and why claiming a profile is not a ranking guarantee. Do not publish until the availability caveats and policy details are rechecked.
